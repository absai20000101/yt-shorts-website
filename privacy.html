Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

YT Shorts Automation is a personal-use application designed to automate video management and private YouTube uploads for its owner.

2. Google User Data Accessed

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access authorised Google services, including:

  • Google account email and basic account information.
  • Google Drive files, folders and file metadata.
  • Video files selected for upload.
  • YouTube video upload permissions.
  • YouTube video identifiers returned after uploads.

Although the Google Drive permission allows broader access, the automation is designed to operate on designated video folders and upload records.

3. How Data Is Used

Google user data is used to:

  • Identify videos awaiting upload.
  • Download selected videos temporarily.
  • Upload videos privately to YouTube.
  • Move successfully uploaded files between folders.
  • Maintain records to prevent duplicate uploads.

4. Data Storage and Security

Original videos and upload records are stored in the owner's Google Drive account.

Temporary video files are downloaded during processing and are intended to be deleted after processing.

OAuth credentials are stored locally or in encrypted automation secrets when cloud automation is enabled. Credentials must not be included in public repositories.

5. Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising, profiling or AI model training.

Data is processed through Google Drive, YouTube and, when enabled, GitHub Actions to provide the authorised automation functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Videos and upload records are retained until the account owner chooses to delete them.

The owner may delete stored credentials, automation secrets, upload records and videos at any time.

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account Connections.

7. Google API Limited Use

YT Shorts Automation's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google API Services User Data Policy

8. YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services.

Users are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

For information about Google's privacy practices, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Children's Privacy

The application may upload educational videos intended for children. However, the automation application itself is operated by its adult owner and does not directly collect personal information from children or YouTube viewers.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
